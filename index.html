What does "+ ad spend" mean?

The $1,700 monthly fee covers management of your advertising campaigns. You choose an additional advertising budget to pay for the actual ads on Meta or Google.

Example: $1,700 management fee + $1,000 ad budget = $2,700 total for that month.

Results vary by business, budget, market, and campaign. No return on investment is guaranteed.
